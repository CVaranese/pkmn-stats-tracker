# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Games tab: the league's six games so far (Weeks 3, 4, 5, 6 and two from Week 8), above the Samples.
- Games tab: the Player filter treats Showdown names the way Showdown does, ignoring case and spaces, so "Nezergy" and "nezergy" are one player.
- Team sheets stay closed when a game opens (from Games, or a saved sheet); only a share link that brings sheets opens them.
- "More detail" opens only that team's card, so the other card no longer jumps. The last choice is still the default for the next game.
- Phones: the first tap on a table header shows what the column means ("… Tap again to sort."), a second tap on the same header sorts. The tap box (headers, luck words, Performance, sprites) now appears under what you tapped (it showed in the top-left corner).
- Phones: the Games list shows each team as two lines of three sprites, the two teams side by side.
- Phones: text boxes and filters use 16px text, so iPhone Safari no longer zooms in when you tap them.
- Phones: the Games list stacks each game (players under each other, buttons below) instead of scrolling sideways; the main tabs fit on one line; team card moves put the damage bar on its own line so move names have room.
- Touch screens: buttons, filters and fold-out headings are taller (44px, small buttons 40px) and table headers easier to tap. Hover highlights only on devices with a mouse, so they don't stay on after a tap.
- Phones: tapping anything that has a hover explanation (column headers, luck words, Performance, sprites) shows it in a small box for 5 seconds.
- Games tab: tap anywhere on a game's row to open it in One game (same as Stats).
- "Hide links and sheets" moved from the tab row (it ran off the screen on phones) to the results toolbar, next to Download CSV. Changing tabs shows the links again.
- "How kills and damage are counted" is now "How each column is counted", right under the table's title instead of at the bottom of every tab.
- New header text: what the site gives (kills, damage, MVPs, luck) and where to start (Games, or a replay link).
- Games tab: a "Bracket" section (closed by default, since it shows results) with the league's Challonge bracket, loaded only when opened.
- Games tab: the three test games are now grouped as "Samples" (were Week 1 / Week 2), so the site can be tried while Showdown is down. League weeks will list above them.
- Team cards: a "Best move" line (the move that did the most damage), and, under "More detail", the Pokémon's moves and damage sources, one line each: times used, a bar for its share of all its damage, damage, KOs (HP with team sheets). Abilities, items, status and weather are named ("Rough Skin (ability)", "Burn (status)"). With a sheet, moves it never used show as "not used".
- Team cards open in a simple view (Kills, Dealt, Taken, Survived, best move); "More detail" adds turns, healing, moves and luck for both cards and is remembered in the browser.
- Team cards: a nicknamed Pokémon shows its nickname as the name, with the species under it (and "Nickname (Species)" in the sprite tooltip).
- With team sheets, damage, hazard damage and healing show in HP only: the team cards drop the % (Dealt, Taken, Healed), and the tables (and Copy for Google Sheets / CSV) drop the Dealt % / Taken % / Hazard % / Healed % / Heal given % columns when every brought Pokémon has the HP number.
- One game: "Copy YouTube title" (e.g. "PPPPP Week 6 Big Large Vs Nezergy") and "Copy thumbnail info" (player names and each team's MVP) buttons under the game line.
- Games tab: "Show results" is now a button like the others (label flips to "Hide results") instead of a plain checkbox. Watch / Stats / Close use the same small button style.
- Games tab is spoiler-free by default: no winner or turn count until "Show results" is ticked (remembered in the browser). Each game shows both teams as small sprites (the six from team preview). Watch now plays the battle at the top of the Games tab, without the stats; Stats opens One game.
- Games tab: the league's games, saved with the site (`site/games/`, added with `tools/add_game.py`), listed by week with a player and week filter. Stats opens a game in One game, loaded from the saved copy, so it works while Showdown is down. `?tab=games` links straight to the list.
- Watch the battle: One game has a "Watch the battle" section that plays the replay in Showdown's own player (needs Showdown online; not in the local version).
- Link previews: sharing the site in Discord (or anywhere that reads Open Graph tags) shows the league banner, title and a one-line description.
- Tabs are now One game, Playoffs (was Several games) and Gambling (a placeholder for now). A "Hide links and sheets" button collapses the input box, for cleaner screenshots.
- Temporary: the saved test game (gen9nationaldex-2705117998) fills in both team sheets when loaded, so HP columns and sets show while Showdown is down.
- When Showdown's move / Pokédex data doesn't load, the site uses its own copies (`site/data/`), so luck and max HP still work while Showdown is down.
- Team summary: switching Pokémon no longer moves anything (the Team MVP, nickname and Mega / Z lines keep their space when empty); Mega tags are black and Z tags porcelain (the urinal colour); the Team totals sit in their own tinted strip with a bigger heading.
- Team summary: click any brought Pokémon on a team to see its stats (Team MVP shown first). Mega / Z-move is a tag on the sprite and a line in that Pokémon's stats ("Mega Evolved on turn 1") instead of a note at the bottom. Luck shows as a die (1 to 5 pips) and a word: Very unlucky, Unlucky, Fair, Lucky, Very lucky; hover for the score. The summary has its own band, "This game", above "Every Pokémon".
- MVP score is now called Performance and counts damage taken (+0.5 per 100%) and turns on field (+0.05 per turn) instead of healing. Team MVP is the best Performance on the team. CSV columns keep their old names (mvp_score, mvp_score_per_game).
- Local version: sprites for the Pokémon in the saved replays are saved in `site/sprites/gen5/`, so they show without Showdown.
- Two tabs: One game (the default: one replay link, the team summary, and a table of that game's Pokémon without the per-game columns already shown above it) and Several games (the links box with Leaderboard / Per game, as before). A share link with one replay opens One game, with more opens Several games.
- Local version: luck and max HP work without Showdown, from copies of its move and Pokédex data in `site/data/` (taken from the pokemon-showdown npm package). Add `?online` to the local address to use Showdown instead.
- Team summary looks plainer: "Team MVP" in normal case, square-cornered Won / Lost labels.
- League theme from the Pinecord Pokémon Pissfest banner: the banner at the top, mint tiled wall behind the page, cream panels with dark outlines, a striped-floor divider and a teal accent; a darker teal version in dark mode.
- Team summary: with exactly one replay loaded, a card per team above the table shows win / loss, the six team-preview sprites (dimmed if not brought, red-outlined if fainted), the Team MVP with its sprite and stats (kills, dealt, taken, healed, turns on field, survived), team totals (kills, lost, dealt, healed, hazards set, switches, crits, player luck) and any Mega / Z-move used. Sprites come from Showdown; if they don't load, initials show instead.
- Local version: when the page is served from this computer (127.0.0.1 / localhost) it never contacts Showdown. Only replays saved in `site/mock/` load, sprites show as initials, and luck / max HP from the Pokédex are unavailable (each says so). The live site is unchanged.
- Column headers have a tooltip saying what the column counts (hover the dotted-underlined name).
- Fix: a team-preview `Zamazenta-*` (or any `-*` species) now matches the base form when that's what was sent out. Before, the base form got a second, "not brought" row in Per game.
- Temporary, during the 2026-10-26 Showdown outage: four Nat Dex replays (gen9nationaldex-2705099074, -2705111047, -2705117998, -2705121899) load from copies bundled with the site, so the page can be tried without Showdown. To be removed.
- When Pokémon Showdown (or Pokepaste) is down or not answering, loading now gives up after 15 s per request with a clear message, instead of hanging or showing a bare "HTTP 522". Once Showdown looks down, the remaining replays are skipped and reported in one line.
- Survival per Pokémon: Turns on field, Survived (standing at the end) and KO'd before moving. Mega / Z-move: Mega + Mega turn, Z-move + Z turn per game; Megas, Mega wins, Z-moves, Z wins in the leaderboard. Also in exports.
- MVP: every brought Pokémon gets an MVP score (+1 per 100% dealt, +1 per kill, +0.5 per 100% healed, +0.5 for surviving) and the top one on each team is Team MVP. Per game: MVP score / Team MVP; leaderboard: MVPs and MVP score / game. Also in exports.
- Luck: multi-hit moves count (number of hits for 2-5 hit moves, every accuracy roll of Triple Axel / Population Bomb; needs the item known, from a sheet or the log). Rolls an unrevealed ability could have changed (Inner Focus, Super Luck, Serene Grace...) are skipped unless a team sheet or the log says which ability it has.
- Luck: per Pokémon Crits / Crits taken, Misses / Dodged, Full paras, Sleep turns, Flinched, Hit itself, Effects landed, and a Luck / Luck against score (each roll = result minus its odds, from Showdown move data); Player luck per game.
- Healing per Pokémon: Healed % (HP it restored to itself: Leftovers, Recover, Rest, drain, Poison Heal, berries, Grassy Terrain…) and Heal given % (to teammates: Wish, Healing Wish, Lunar Dance, Heal Pulse, Z-move heals). Healed HP / Heal given HP with team sheets. In the leaderboard, the Per game table and exports.
- Switches per Pokémon: Switches (by choice), Pivots (U-turn, Volt Switch, Flip Turn, Parting Shot…), Forced out (Roar, Whirlwind, Dragon Tail, Red Card, Eject Button, Emergency Exit) and In after KO. In the leaderboard, the Per game table and exports.
- Hidden Zoroark: games where a team brought a Zoroark that never dropped its disguise are listed under the links box and marked `hidden_zoroark` in Per game rows, since its stats sit on the Pokémon it copied.
- Hazards: per Pokémon, damage done through Stealth Rock / Spikes / Steelsurge (Hazard %, and Hazard HP with team sheets; part of Dealt, not on top), KOs from hazards (part of Passive), hazards set (each layer) and hazards removed from its own side (Defog, Rapid Spin...); per player, the turns hazards were up on the opponent's side. In the leaderboard (except turns), the Per game table and exports.
- Fix: after Court Change, damage and KOs from the swapped hazards go to the Court Change user (they credited nobody before).
- Per game rows now say how the game ended (`end_reason`: normal, forfeit, timer, tie or unfinished) and how many turns it lasted, so short timer losses and forfeits can be filtered out in the spreadsheet. Shown as "Ended by" and "Turns" in the Per game table and its exports.
- Damage dealt and taken per Pokémon, in % of max HP, credited like kills (direct hits to the attacker; burn, hazards, weather, Leech Seed, Rocky Helmet and so on to their cause). Overkill doesn't count; self-inflicted damage (recoil, Life Orb, confusion, Substitute's cost) counts as taken with no dealer. With team sheets, also in raw HP (% × the target's max HP; about ±1% of max HP per hit, since replays round to whole %). New columns in the leaderboard, the Per game table and their exports.
- Team sheets (optional): add a Pokepaste link or a Showdown export per team. Each sheet is matched to every game where that team played (all six Pokémon at team preview) and adds Max HP, item, ability and moves to the Per game table and its exports. Pokepaste sheets are included in share links (`&t=`). Text copied straight off a Pokepaste page (with its ``` lines) works too.
- Web page (`site/`) for GitHub Pages: paste replay links, get a kill/death leaderboard per player + Pokémon (or per Pokémon) and a per-game table; sortable columns, player filter.
- Copy for Google Sheets (tab-separated) and Download CSV, both exporting the current view.
- Shareable links: `?r=id1,id2,...` opens the page with those replays loaded; "Copy share link" button.
- Parser ported from Python to JavaScript (`site/parser.js`) with the same draft-league kill rules; supports singles, doubles, Random Battles (no team preview), bo3 game replays, smogtours and private (`...pw`) replay IDs.
- Failed replays (404, private, unreadable) are listed by ID instead of stopping the run.
- Fix: Zoroark's Illusion no longer mis-credits stats. When the disguise breaks, kills, the KO on Zoroark and status it inflicted while disguised move from the copied Pokémon to Zoroark; the copied Pokémon keeps its own stats. (A disguise that never breaks still can't be detected.)
