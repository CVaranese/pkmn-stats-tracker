- Hidden Zoroark: when a team's Zoroark never drops its disguise, the site now works out which stays on the field it could have been. A stay is ruled out when a move hit it (that breaks Illusion) or when Zoroark can't learn a move it used. A stay is proven to be Zoroark when the Pokémon shown can't learn a move it used (a "Donphan" using Shadow Ball), or when the HP and status don't carry over between switches (left at 60%, came back at 100% without Regenerator). Proven stays move to Zoroark on their own: in Week 6 nezergy vs ElkG Big Large, the two Donphan stays that used Shadow Ball and U-turn are now Zoroark-Hisui, which gets the Rotom-Heat KO. The notice under the links lists every stay that could have been Zoroark and what it's counted as. The league admin makes the other picks: on the local site each stay has a Move to Zoroark / Move back button to try a pick out, and `python -I tools/set_illusion.py <game> <p1|p2> <stays|none|auto>` rolls it out in site/games/index.json (`illusion`), where it overrides the proven stays. Move lists come from Showdown's learnsets (site/data/learnsets.json, `tools/learnsets.py`).
